Cook together, anywhere

Every recipe, localized

Browse chapters, search ingredients, and cook step-by-step in your own language — from Arabic and Urdu to Japanese and Swahili, with full right-to-left layouts. Works with Android's per-app language setting.

Kids in the kitchen

A bright, illustrated Kids mode with get-ready checklists, allergen banners, grown-up alerts, swipeable step cards that keep the screen awake, and a celebration when the dish is done.

Every Android screen

Bottom navigation on phones, a navigation rail on foldables and small tablets, and a full sidebar on large tablets and Chromebooks — with two-column recipes, keyboard shortcuts, mouse hover and right-click menus, and live resizing in split-screen and desktop windows.

Accessible by design

TalkBack labels and headings, large touch targets, text that follows your font size, strong colour contrast, predictive back, and animations that respect "Remove animations".

Watch and learn

Recipe videos play right inside the app through privacy-enhanced YouTube embeds, or jump out to YouTube with one tap.

Open links in the app

Links to recipes on fifi.cooking open straight in the app when it's installed. Share any recipe with the Android share sheet.

Inside the app

Phone, 7-inch and 10-inch tablet — English and Arabic.
