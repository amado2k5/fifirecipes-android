Privacy Policy

Effective: 30 September 2026 · FiFi Recipes for Android (package cooking.fifi.android, "the app"), published by Dr. Fatma / FiFi.

The short version

We collect no data. No accounts, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. In Google Play's Data safety section the app declares that it collects no data and shares no data. The only permission it uses is Internet access.

What the app does on your device

  • Downloads recipe content (JSON and images) from fifi.cooking over HTTPS.
  • Stores one setting on your device: your chosen language (also shown in Android's per-app language settings). It may be included in your own Android device backup, which is controlled by you and Google, not us. Ingredient ticks and your place in a recipe are kept only while the app is open.
  • Caches recipe data and images on-device so the app loads faster.

What fifi.cooking sees

Like any website, the static hosting that serves the recipe data may log standard request metadata (IP address, timestamp, requested file) for security and abuse prevention. No recipe content request carries any account, identifier, or personal data — there is nothing to attach it to.

YouTube embeds

Some recipes include cooking videos hosted on YouTube. If you choose to play one, the app loads an embedded player from youtube-nocookie.com — Google's privacy-enhanced embed domain, which avoids setting tracking cookies until you press play. At that point Google's privacy policy applies to the player itself. Choosing "Open in YouTube" instead hands the link to the YouTube app or your browser.

Opening links

Links to fifi.cooking recipes can open in the app (Android App Links). Android verifies this through a public file on fifi.cooking; no information about you is exchanged.

Children

Kids mode is a feature for families cooking together — the app collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

Any future change to this policy will be posted here before it ships.

Your rights and contact

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete — uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage in Android Settings) removes everything it stored on your device. Questions: android@fifi.cooking.
