TITLE
FiFi Recipes

SHORT DESCRIPTION
2,300+ family recipes in 25 languages, plus a Kids cooking mode.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Dr. Fatma's beloved Egyptian home cooking, on your phone and tablet.

FiFi Recipes brings the complete fifi.cooking archive — more than a thousand family recipes — to Android, in 25 languages. Browse by chapter, search by ingredient, and cook step by step with clear, numbered instructions.

EVERY RECIPE, IN YOUR LANGUAGE
• 25 languages, from Arabic and English to Urdu, Japanese, Swahili and more
• Full right-to-left layouts for Arabic, Urdu, Persian, Pashto and Hebrew
• Beautiful type for every script, including Nastaliq for Urdu
• Works with Android's per-app language setting

COOK WITH CONFIDENCE
• Tick off ingredients as you gather them
• Numbered steps, alternative methods and handy tips
• "From Fatma's notebook" — the story and culture behind each dish
• Estimated calories, protein, carbs and fat
• Tap the photo to zoom in on the details
• Watch recipe videos right in the app

KIDS IN THE KITCHEN
A bright, illustrated Kids mode for families cooking together: a get-ready checklist (wash hands, apron, grown-up nearby), tickable ingredients with drawings, allergen warnings, big swipeable step cards that keep the screen awake, grown-up alerts for the tricky parts, and a celebration when the dish is done.

MADE FOR EVERY ANDROID SCREEN
• Phones, foldables, tablets and Chromebooks — the layout adapts as you fold, rotate or resize
• Two-column recipes on large screens
• Keyboard shortcuts, mouse and trackpad support
• TalkBack, large text and high contrast friendly

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking, no data collected. Just recipes.

An internet connection is required to load recipes from fifi.cooking.
